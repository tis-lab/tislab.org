Ginnie is a scientific applications engineer, data modeler, and internationally recognized expert on Adverse Outcome Pathways (AOPs). Her background spans bioinformatics, immunology, science education, and the arts, giving her a distinctive perspective on multi-scale modeling of biological mechanisms. She currently works on the Monarch Initiative and BioData Catalyst.
