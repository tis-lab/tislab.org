Chris is a graduate student in the Genetics and Molecular Biology Program at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, pursuing his PhD with TISLab. His interdisciplinary research background spans environmental and geospatial science, biomedical engineering and immunology, molecular biology and microbial genomics, and biomedical informatics. His PhD research aims to develop knowledge-based resources and clinical decision-support tools to improve differential diagnosis, with a focus on rare diseases.

- BA, Environmental Studies, College of the Holy Cross, Worcester, MA, 2022
